SEC 01 Who We Are

A Tool Room Built on Drawings, Not Promises

Founded in 2020, JMC Engineering is an MSME-registered precision tool room in Padi — Chennai's historic tooling belt. We design and build jigs, fixtures, press tools, plastic moulds and gauges for automotive and industrial manufacturers across India.

Our CEO brings 30+ years in the tool & SPM domain — 12 years in Singapore and 18 in Chennai — covering die and tool design, R&D and engineering finance. Every project starts with a free feasibility review and ends with full documentation and post-delivery support.
