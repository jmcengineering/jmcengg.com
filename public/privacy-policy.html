Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026 · Applies to jmcengg.com

The short version. We collect what you type into our enquiry form and the drawings you attach, and we use them only to quote and build your tooling. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not share your drawings with anyone outside JMC Engineering. You can ask us to delete everything at any time by emailing info@jmcengg.com.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not run advertising or behavioural tracking cookies on this site.
  • We do not send marketing emails to people who only asked for a quotation.
  • We do not show your drawings, part names or company name as a case study or on social media without asking you first, in writing.

4. Your drawings and confidentiality

We treat every drawing you send as commercially confidential. Access is limited to the JMC Engineering staff who need it to quote or manufacture your tooling. We do not pass drawings to other customers, and where we use an outside vendor for an operation we cannot perform in-house, we share only the minimum needed for that operation.

If your organisation requires a signed non-disclosure agreement before you send drawings, email us and we will sign yours before you upload anything.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become orders — kept for up to 24 months, then deleted, so we can pick up a conversation you restart.
  • Enquiries that become orders — kept for 8 years alongside the job records, because tax, GST and product-liability obligations require it.
  • Drawings — kept for as long as we may need to remanufacture or service the tool, and deleted on your written request unless a job record requires us to keep them.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold and what we have done with it;
  • correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase your data where we are not required to keep it;
  • withdraw a consent you previously gave;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to.

Email info@jmcengg.com with the subject line DATA REQUEST and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider keeps ordinary server access logs (IP address, browser, page requested) for security and reliability. Google Fonts receives a request from your browser when the page loads so it can serve the typefaces. The location map in our contact section is embedded from Google Maps; when you scroll far enough for it to load, your browser contacts Google and Google may set its own cookies. If you would rather it did not, our address is written out in full beside the map and you can look it up separately.

10. Children

This is a business-to-business website. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data — for example when we move the enquiry form off FormSubmit — we will update this page and change the date at the top.
